    Roadmap to the platinum

    One playthrough, no online trophies, about 200 hours, difficulty 4/10. Do it in three stages — story, 100% completion, cleanup — and keep a manual save at the start and end of every chapter: there is no mission replay.

    The 100% completion checklist (A Long Road)

    Open Progress > Stats > 100% Completion after the story. Only the numbers below count — most categories ask for a portion of the total, so stop when the checklist says so.

      Where the facts come from

        What is still unconfirmed

        Three hidden trophies have no confirmed unlock method in any guide yet — Bad Luck, I Guess, The Truth Is Out There — and three more are only partly confirmed (In The Beginning…, Capital Offense, Ten Pin). Their entries say so plainly and give the best current reading instead of inventing a method. Actions Speak Louder (500 Criminal Profile actions) rests on a single source, GTABase, and is marked as such. This guide will be corrected as the sources confirm more.
